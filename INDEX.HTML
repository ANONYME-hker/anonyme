One month · 1 September

Saphinael

I wrote you something. Open it slowly.

Tap the seal

Happy one month, my love.

The letter

Just for you

My dearest Saphinael,

Open when…

Little envelopes, for every moment

Keep them close. Open them whenever your heart needs me.

Since 1 September

Every second I have loved you

And the counter never stops. Neither do I.

My promise

Hold the seal, and it is yours

Press and hold for two seconds.

You are beautiful, you are loved, and you are mine. Happy one month. — Othniel
